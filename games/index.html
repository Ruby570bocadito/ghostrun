Play the test games yourself

Use a computer: the games are played with the keyboard.

Each link opens a game that records your own playtest (where you go, where you die, whether you finish, how long it takes) so it can be compared with the ghosts. Nothing is recorded unless you open a game from this page. Play each one until you finish or until you would give up in real life, then press I give up or just close the tab.

    Opened links turn grey. Play the version as designed first: every one of them can be finished. The version with the planted flaw has one design flaw on purpose, so that Ghostrun has something known to find; some of them cannot be finished at all, on purpose.
